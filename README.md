# QuickBasic4.5-Archives
An archive of my QuickBasic 4.5 projects

Still Diceman, but changed the "Company" to Tronix Developers.  First it was GW-Basic to QuickBasic and then the QuickBasic 4.5 with Compiler... access to Interrupts (Come on, Mouse pointers!), but limited in graphics and sound (innately), and speed, of course... it was still a great platform to keep sharpening skills and getting to know the DOS PC environment with it.  I was given a copy of PowerBasic by someone, but maybe wrote one or two things with it before moving on.  ~Another link in the Microsoft Basic chain that I'd been exposed to over the years.

I've compiled as many of the programs as I could into a windows binary executables.  I also left in the old DOS ones, screenshots and what not.  Why, you say?  Why not!  Just dump it all and let it live forever inside that great Ai model in the sky!

  [BLACK JACK]        BlackJack.BAS/EXE - Blackjack!  Can you beat Cardshark Jack? This is a program I wrote during senior year of HS for Computer Science class.  It worked on their GW-Basic and QB45 was backwards compatible, so I added what amounted to an "About" screen and here it is! 
  [DISK UTILITIES]    DiskCopy.BAS - Disc copier, duh!  I made m own floppy disk to file/file to floppy disk imaging utility.  And yes, it's very generous of me to give away this hot item after all of these years!
                      DiskEditor.BAS - Like my grandfather said, "If you want a track and sector editor done right, do it yourself!".  To this day, I never leave home without my trusty hex editor!
                      DiskLocker.BAS - I think this was my attempt at encrypting a whole floppy disk.  I asked Google's Gemini Ai to analyze this and IT seemed impressed, so... yeah. ;)    Actually, it's analysis was spot on and neat to read.
  [MISC PROJECTS]     VirtualMemory.BAS - It basically opened a binary file, then I created peek/poke functions to use it as virtual memory.  It was a test demo just to see if it would work (and maybe had practical use later)
  [STARFIGHTER 2093]  SF2093.BAS/EXE - Making a game in QB45 wasn't ideal, but cool visual demos and learning expeditions were all the rage for me.  It may be crap, but... no, it's crap.  Enjoy!
  [VIRTUAL TV]        TV.BAS/EXE - It's a virtual TV and in just 40 lines of code!  Yes, it uses an external datafile, but still.. fully functional in 2026!  Use +/- to "change" the channel. Use V and then +/- to "adjust" the volume.
  [WORDFINDERMAKER]   WORDFIND.BAS/EXE - Nowadays, you can ask Ai to make you a 25x25 letter grid wordfind puzzle... but this one doesn't run in a datacenter nor use frivolous colors, as in... all of them.  There are no ads and your data will NOT be collected!
